Our Story

Truth is our starting point

The name Veritaz draws on veritas, the Latin word for truth. It reflects how we work: accurate figures, honest advice and a clear view of where your business stands.

Based in Kandana, we serve businesses that want more than a number-cruncher. We offer accounting, audit, taxation, company secretarial, payroll, business process outsourcing and legal support, so you do not need to juggle several providers.

Our emblem says it well: the pillar stands for stability and the law, the compass arrow for direction and strategy, and the shield for the trust you place in us.
